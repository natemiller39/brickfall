You get 3 of every tower, already at max level, and unlimited free walls. Foes take the shortest way from the red gate to your castle, so lay walls to stretch that route into a maze; the dashed line shows it and turns amber to preview a change. You can't seal it off completely. Place everything, press Start, and the waves come back to back, harder every time. Towers lock when you start, but walls can be built and removed anytime, even mid-wave. The game saves after every wave; Pause lets you save and quit. Tanks and Flamethrowers do only 35% to flyers, and Air Defense only 35% to ground foes. Rifle shots and Gas ignore armor. The Laser aims nearby but its beam crosses the whole field. How far you get is your score.

Combos

Shatter · Freeze + Tank. A frozen foe hit by a tank shell takes 2.5× damage.
Ignite · Gas + Flamethrower. Fire that touches a gas cloud makes it explode and sets everything inside burning.
Fused Beam · Laser + Laser. Lasers placed side by side link up; each neighbor adds 60% damage and a thicker beam.
Supercharge · Freeze + Electric. Electric does 1.75× to slowed or frozen foes.
Marked · Rifle + Machine Gun. A rifle hit marks a foe for 4 seconds; machine guns do +60% to it.

Flyers (✈, with a shadow under them) ignore your maze and head straight for the castle. Bosses every 5th wave, in order: Dragon, Mothership, Star Cruiser, Hive Queen, then they return tougher. They shoot your towers to jam them, bricks fall off as they take damage, and each takes much less from one kind of tower: the Dragon fire, the Mothership lasers and electric, the Star Cruiser guns, the Hive Queen gas.
Find the combination. Nothing is immune, but every invader takes less from some towers and more from others; RESISTS or WEAK! pops over its head when you hit it. From wave 4, waves carry traits like Fireproof, Plated or Frostproof, shown in the bar before the wave starts, so plan the mix of towers that beats it. Later waves stack two or three traits.
