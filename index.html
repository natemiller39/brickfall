Speed bonus: clear a level Fast, Medium or Slow for a bonus (the top right shows which one you're on and how long is left). Lava levels: lava sits behind the wall. Breaking a dark cracked brick spills molten drops, and each one that hits your paddle counts as a hit. Bosses every 3rd level, in order: Dragon, Mothership, Star Cruiser, Hive Queen, then they return tougher. Break them apart for triple points, a huge bonus and +1 life. Your paddle survives 3 hits before you lose a life. Knock out the gold core and the boss enrages. The board shows bosses beaten as B. Bomb bricks (some levels): 2–3 bombs on the bottom row. Break each one before the shared timer runs out; get them all for a big bonus. ★ Jackpot block: pays more every hit and vanishes when you lose a ball.
You score for every point of damage you deal, plus a knockout bonus. Bigger creatures gain a hit every 6 levels; Buzzers and Fireflies always go down in one and points grow with your combo.
